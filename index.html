Continuous evolution

We don't build a tool and walk away. We actively study emerging neuroscience and update Ollin to reflect how the ADHD brain actually works — not how society wishes it worked.

Vetted by professionals

We regularly interview clinicians, therapists, and ADHD specialists so our non-pharmaceutical tools — the 1 PM rescue, the spatial context rooms — stay grounded in proven cognitive frameworks.

Accountable to our community

Clinical research means nothing if it doesn't survive the real world. We listen, we test, and we refuse to ship anything that induces guilt, shame, or notification fatigue.

Three flagship features
What's inside, day one.

Not another chatbot. Ask one question and Ollin answers from everything it's holding for you — your notes, decisions, open questions, next steps. No re-explaining, no prompt engineering, no conversation to manage. It's AI designed around executive function: it does the remembering so the answer meets you where you actually are.

One question · full context · one grounded answer

You don't need a perfect plan — you need one that fits the day you're actually having. Tell Ollin what's in your head and it gives you back something simple enough to follow, soft enough to forgive you. Yours, not the app's. And if today gets away from you, you just come back.

Dump → day · realistic blocks · nothing forgotten

One tap — or one honest sentence — and Ollin triages the moment: can't start, too much, it broke, or just tired. Then it shrinks the wall to nothing — "Open your email. Now type one word." Some days it just asks for a ridiculous sentence; the laugh is a tiny dopamine hit that turns the engine over. Miss a day and you come back to a warm welcome, not a wall of red — just an honest "want to carry this into today?"
